What's new in Inflation Explainer AI

Version 4 (November 2026): three new tools, added by the ByteBusters team at Yewa College, Ilaro, Ogun State. They work on a basic phone, need no account, and keep your prices on your own phone.

📊 My own inflation this month

Type what you buy, how many a month, and last month's and this month's price. Our code works out your own inflation, shows your biggest movers and explains the result in plain English. It can fill itself from your price diary. If you like, the AI can explain it in one of 17 Nigerian languages (only item names and numbers are sent).

📒 Price diary

Save market prices over time. Each item gets its own small line chart (tap a dot to see the date and price) and a table. Download everything as a CSV file for Excel, Google Sheets or a class project, and load it again on another phone.

📣 Share card

One tap makes a picture that says "My basket went up X% this month" with your top movers, ready for your WhatsApp status, plus a ready-made WhatsApp message.

Your data: all of it stays in your phone's browser storage. We never see your prices, and we never make up prices: every number comes from you.
