How it works

  1. You type an item, its type, two prices and your location.
  2. Price check: if you chose a location, our code compares your price with real price records. A clear typing slip (like an extra zero) is corrected and shown to you. Without a location, your prices are never changed.
  3. Our code does the maths: the difference, percent change, monthly change and what ₦1,000 is worth now. The AI never calculates.
  4. Privacy filter: phone numbers, emails, account or ID numbers and names after "my name is" are removed.
  5. The AI explains using only our numbers, Nigeria's figures and your state's figures from the Price Watch board.

About this website

Inflation Explainer AI was built by secondary school students at Yewa College, Ilaro, in the ByteBusters Generative AI Team. Our families keep asking why prices go up so fast, so we built a tool that turns two prices into a clear story anyone can understand.

Our guardrails

  • The AI may only use the numbers we give it. It cannot make up statistics.
  • The maths is done by code, not by the AI.
  • Answers stay short and in simple English.
  • No sign-up. Nothing is saved except your light or night mode choice. No personal details go in or come out.
