Why is the price of animal and poultry feed going up in Nigeria?

Quick answer: Feed costs more when maize and soybean are scarce or dear, since they are its main ingredients.

The 4 main reasons

  1. The seasonIt is cheapest just after harvest, when markets are full, and dearest months later when stocks run low. This happens every year.
  2. The cost of making itFertiliser, seeds, animal feed, packaging and spare parts have become dearer. Producers pass that cost on.
  3. Insecurity in farming areasWhen farmers cannot safely reach their farms, less is planted and harvested. Less supply with the same demand means a higher price.
  4. The exchange rateIt is imported, or made with imported materials or machines. Importers pay in dollars, so when the naira is weaker each dollar costs more naira and the shelf price rises.

One thing to know

Feed price explains much of the price of eggs, chicken, fish and pork.

Will the price of animal and poultry feed come down?

Prices of animal and poultry feed fall when the things that pushed them up ease: the season, the cost of making it, insecurity in farming areas. Because it is seasonal, it usually gets cheaper around harvest.
