Why is the price of palm oil going up in Nigeria?

Quick answer: Palm oil costs more in the low production season, when processing and transport cost more, and when the world price is high.

The 4 main reasons

  1. The seasonIt is cheapest just after harvest, when markets are full, and dearest months later when stocks run low. This happens every year.
  2. Electricity and generatorsFactories, mills, bakeries and cold rooms need power. When they run on diesel or pay more for electricity, the cost goes into the product.
  3. Fuel and transportAlmost everything in Nigeria travels by road. When petrol or diesel costs more, every lorry trip from farm or port to market costs more, and that is added to the price.
  4. World pricesIt is traded around the world. When the world price rises, or a big exporting country sells less, Nigeria pays more too.

One thing to know

Palm fruit is harvested more in some months than others, so the price follows a yearly rhythm.

What a family can do

  • Buy more when it is in season and store it well.
  • Buy in bulk with neighbours or relatives and share: a bag costs less per cup than a cup at a time.
  • Compare two or three sellers before buying; prices differ even in the same market.
  • Buy a larger container in the peak season; it keeps well.

Will the price of palm oil come down?

Prices of palm oil fall when the things that pushed them up ease: the season, electricity and generators, fuel and transport. Because it is seasonal, it usually gets cheaper around harvest.
