How it works

How do I work out my own inflation for this month?

List the things you buy, how many of each you buy in a month, and the price last month and this month. Multiply each price by how many you buy, add them up for each month, then take (this month − last month) ÷ last month × 100. My Price Diary does this maths for you on your phone.

Why is my own inflation different from the NBS figure?

NBS measures the average price change of many items across Nigeria, usually compared with the same month last year. Your number uses only the things you buy, your own prices, and one month of change, so it can be higher or lower.

Is my price diary private?

Yes. Your prices are saved only on your phone. Nothing is sent to us unless you press "Explain with AI", which sends only the item names and numbers. You can download your diary as a CSV file.

This is simple arithmetic on prices you typed, not an official figure. Official rates come from the National Bureau of Statistics (NBS); see the latest on our home page.
